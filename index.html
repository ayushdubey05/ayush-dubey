THE WORK

Everything a website needs, handled by one person.

Every project starts the same way — with a conversation, not a form. I ask what the site actually needs to do, who it needs to speak to, and what it can safely leave out, then I sketch the shape of it before a single line of code is written. From there I design the whole thing myself: the layout, the type, the colour, the way one section leads into the next, so the site feels considered rather than assembled from parts.

Once the design is settled, I build it by hand — clean, fast-loading pages that work properly on a phone as well as a laptop, forms that actually deliver mail, and a structure search engines can read without trouble. If you need people to buy something, book something, or sign up for something, that gets built in from the start rather than bolted on afterward.

And when the site is live, the work doesn't stop there. I host it, keep it secure, make small edits when your business changes, and I'm reachable directly — on whichever platform you already use — rather than hidden behind a support ticket. That's the whole offer: one person, doing the entire job, at a cost that makes sense for a small business or an independent brand.
