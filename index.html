ABOUT AYUSHRUGS

Preserving Rajasthan's Weaving Traditions

Founded in 1978, Ayushrugs supports traditional artisans across Rajasthan by bringing hand-knotted, natural-dye rugs directly to global homes without industrial middlemen.

Every rug is made on a traditional loom by a named weaver. We pay fair wages, use only natural dyes and undyed wool, and never rush the process. A single 6×9 rug can take six weeks of careful hand-knotting.

The craft isn't just about creating a beautiful object — it's about preserving a centuries-old tradition and ensuring that weaving families can continue their work with dignity and fair compensation.

Why hand-knotting still matters 6wks

The average time one weaver spends on a single 6×9 rug.

A machine can print a rug pattern in minutes. A weaver ties every knot by hand, row by row, checking density and tension until the rug is done. It's slow work. It's precise work. It's why a hand-knotted rug can last generations.

RUG CARE GUIDE

How to care for your hand-knotted rug

Everyday cleaning

Vacuum regularly on a low setting without the beater bar. Shake outdoor runners outdoors. Spot-clean spills immediately with a clean, damp cloth — blot, never rub.

Spills & stains

Blot liquid with a white cloth. For food or oil, sprinkle baking soda, leave 15 minutes, then vacuum. Avoid harsh chemicals and bleach.

Rotation

Rotate your rug every 6–12 months so wear and sunlight are even. This is especially important for high-traffic areas and rooms with strong light.

Professional cleaning

Have the rug professionally cleaned every 1–3 years depending on use. Tell the cleaner it's hand-knotted natural-dye wool so they use the correct method.

Storage

If storing, roll (never fold) the rug with the pile facing inward. Wrap in cotton or breathable fabric. Avoid plastic bags for long periods.

Padding

Use a good quality rug pad. It protects the floor, prevents slipping, and extends the life of the rug by reducing friction.

Need help? WhatsApp us a photo of any stain or damage and we'll advise the best next step.
